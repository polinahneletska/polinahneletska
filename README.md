# Polina Hneletska

Founder & Research Director, [AVBR Lab](https://avbrlab.org) (American Visual & Behavioral Research Lab Inc.) — an independent nonprofit research lab in California that audits algorithmic bias in e-commerce recommendation systems from the outside: what the algorithms push, whom they show, and whom they leave out.

Fifteen years on both sides of the retail shelf: beauty retail and planograms executed by hand since 2011, field audits of shelf placement across a national FMCG market, brand design, and her own commercial product photography studio in Dnipro, Ukraine. In 2026 she founded AVBR Lab to audit the algorithmic shelf the same way she once audited the physical one — from the outside, with open protocols anyone can rerun.

- **Research:** price steering, popularity bias, brand concentration, visual representation, accessibility — black-box audits with open, reproducible protocols and no platform cooperation
- **Program:** Shopify as the first wave (300 stores, August 2026), BigCommerce as the second platform (census, August 2026), longitudinal waves scheduled through 2027
- **Protocol:** [avbrlab/audit-protocol](https://github.com/avbrlab/audit-protocol) (CC BY 4.0)
